Planner PDF printing questions

Can I resize an A5 planner PDF to Half Letter?

Yes. Select Half Letter and Fit whole design. An A5 design scales to about 94.39%, keeping its proportions with space above and below. A5 is wider than Half Letter, so Actual size cannot fit it into that narrower frame. See the dimensions and worked example.

Why are my double-sided planner pages not lining up?

Start with a test sheet. If the 100 mm ruler is the wrong length, check scaling first. If the back repeats the same shift, measure it upright and enter that observed offset. Varying shifts or skew cannot be corrected with one X/Y value. Follow the alignment checks.

Will this remove white borders from my printer?

No. Printer margins and borderless modes depend on the printer, paper and driver. You can place a smaller insert on A4 or Letter paper and cut around it, provided the design and trim marks fit your printable area.

Can I print Personal-size inserts on standard paper?

Use Custom trim and enter your own insert measurements, within the tool’s supported range of 60–210 mm wide and 100–260 mm high. The exported full sheet is A4 or Letter, with one insert per side for cutting. This does not enable automatic duplex on a small paper size your printer rejects.

What happens when my planner PDF has an odd number of pages?

Duplex adds a final blank when needed: a seven-page file becomes eight output sides on four sheets. Original blank pages stay. If the first source page is a back, choose that setting to add a blank front. Single-sided mode does not add pairing blanks.

Is the tool free, and do I have to upload my PDF?

Yes. Resize and export individual PDFs for free, including double-sided output and test sheets. No account, watermark or export cooldown.
