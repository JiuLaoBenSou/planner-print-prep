Questions, a confusing page order, or a print that is still shifted? Write to fanghong1994@gmail.com.

Useful details to include

Your device and browser version, paper and trim size, single/manual/automatic duplex, flip edge, and whether the ruler measures 100 mm. The tool can prepare a settings-only feedback summary for you to copy into an email.

Please do not send private PDFs or purchased templates. A description or your own minimal example is usually enough. Sending an email is your choice; nothing is submitted automatically by the tool.
