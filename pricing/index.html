Pricing: free Core and Plus v1

Finish an individual PDF for free. Plus adds convenience when you repeat the same print setup.

Core — free

One multi-page PDF, sizing, binding space, safe-area checks, front/back order, test sheets, fixed offsets and all single-document exports. No account, watermark or export cooldown. Settings JSON import and export remain free.

Plus v1 — US$9 once

No subscription or automatic renewal. Includes up to 10 PDFs in a sequential batch (20 MiB each, 80 MiB total), up to 20 named settings profiles, and a ZIP containing per-document output and an error/warning report. Manual duplex includes separate Full, Fronts and Backs files for each document. Output is capped at 64 MiB per batch.

Plus is not available to purchase yet. The free Core is ready to use now; no payment details are required.

Delivery and restoration

When sales open, pay through Creem and return to the tool. Plus opens after our server confirms payment. No registration form, password or activation code is needed. On another browser, enter your purchase email and open the one-time sign-in link. You can keep this device signed in or uncheck that option on a shared computer. Access checks require internet; PDFs stay in your browser. Saved profiles remain local to each browser unless you export a backup.

What a purchase does not include

No planner templates, printer hardware, remote printing, physical calibration guarantee, team resale rights, or promise of all future major versions. Source templates remain subject to their own licenses.
